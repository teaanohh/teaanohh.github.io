About

A designer who can build

I started in UI/UX and media design at Nanyang Polytechnic, where I led a team making an educational mini-game for children with learning difficulties. That project showed me how much design decisions change the way people play, so I moved into game development at SIT–DigiPen.

Now I design mechanics and levels, write the documentation that explains them, and implement them in C++. I care about game rules feeling fair. For example, enemies in The Last Act can't hurt the shadow, because the shadow isn't something physical.

Outside of games, I edit videos and make motion graphics. I speak English and Mandarin.
