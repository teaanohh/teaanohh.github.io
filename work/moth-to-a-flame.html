Game

Moth to a Flame

A side-scrolling platformer where a blue moth braves a shadowy forest to find its mate before the light around it disappears.

My role
Gameplay Programmer
Team
4 people
Built with
C, CProcessing
Timeline
Oct – Dec 2024 · 2 difficulty modes
  1. 1What I changed
  2. 2What happened

Stage 1 What I changed

Quest A · Idea

A stamina system inspired by Flappy Bird

I took inspiration from Flappy Bird, where staying in the air takes constant effort.

Choice

Flying drains stamina, and when it runs out the moth falls. Players have to choose between flying and crawling along the ground and platforms.

Stage 2 What happened

Quest A · Shipped

A stamina system inspired by Flappy Bird

Flight, crawl, dash and gravity, plus the stamina and health UI, in both Easy and Hard modes.

What else I built

  • Controls: A and D to fly left or right, both together to fly up, click-and-hold to crawl, and space to dash.
  • Level: the forest layout, with ground tiles, trees and branch platforms.
  • Win condition: reaching the moth's mate, plus the game-complete screen.
